Brushline — privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026. Publisher: sogerg enterprise — contact: sogerg.enterprise@gmail.com.

Only the official prefectural or municipal order is binding. Brushline produces an indicative outline; it is neither legal advice nor an official document.

In short

No account, no ads, no analytics. Your properties, plots, drawn buildings, neighbours, dates and photos stay on your phone.

What is stored on the phone

  • Properties, the computed outline, each neighbour’s status and dates: in the app’s local database (encrypted in the published version; the key is kept in the phone’s secure keychain).
  • Before / after photos: copied into the app’s private folder, with their date and, if you turn it on, location and direction.
  • The chosen language and reminder settings.

“Erase all my data”, in Settings, deletes all of this. Uninstalling the app does too.

What goes over the network

  • Town packages (land register and zoning, open public data): when the service is online, the app downloads the list of towns and the package of the chosen town. The server therefore sees which town is requested and the connection’s IP address, like any website; it receives neither your address, nor your plots, nor your photos.
  • Today’s fire danger: the app downloads the day’s file for your country; it contains the levels of all départements and does not depend on you.
  • These files are served by Cloudflare (host). The publisher keeps no personal logs.
  • Links to official texts open in your browser.
  • Letter templates and the PDF file are created on the phone; you choose whether to share them.

This version sends no notifications from a server: reminders are scheduled on the phone.

Permissions

  • Camera and photos: take or choose before / after photos.
  • Location (while in use only, optional): centre the plan on your house and record where a photo was taken. It is never sent.
  • Compass: record which way a photo faces.
  • Notifications: deadline reminders scheduled on the phone.

Reviews

The app may show the system review prompt (App Store or Google Play); that prompt is handled by Apple or Google.

Data sources

Land register: DGFiP / Etalab, Licence Ouverte 2.0. Clearing zoning: IGN, Licence Ouverte 2.0. Fire danger: Météo-France (Météo des forêts), Licence Ouverte 2.0.

Children

The app is meant for property owners and is not directed at children.

Your rights

The publisher holds no personal data about you. Questions: sogerg.enterprise@gmail.com.
